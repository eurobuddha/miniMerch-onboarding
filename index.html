Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about miniMerch and Minima

Minima is a Layer-1 blockchain that runs completely on-device. Unlike other blockchains that require powerful servers, Minima nodes can run on phones, IoT devices, or desktop computers. This makes it perfect for truly decentralized applications like miniMerch.

For buying and browsing: No, you can use public nodes or access Minima through other apps.

For selling with miniMerch: Yes, your node needs to be online to receive orders. However, Minima's mobile app runs efficiently in the background with minimal battery drain.

Anything legal! miniMerch supports physical goods (with postal delivery), digital products (with email/URL delivery), and services. You set the shipping options, prices, and product details.

Customers can pay with USDT (a stablecoin pegged to the US Dollar) or Minima (the native token). Payments go directly to your Minima address - no intermediary, no payment processor, no escrow.

Yes! Unlike Shopify or other platforms where your store can be deleted by the company, your miniMerch shop is tied to your Minima node. As long as you run a node, your shop exists. There's no company that can shut you down.

Buyers see your shop as a simple web page (a MiniDapp). They select their product, choose shipping, enter their delivery address, and pay. Your delivery address is encrypted and only accessible by you - buyers can't see each other's addresses, and you can't see buyer's other information.
