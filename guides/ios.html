🍎 iOS Setup Guide

Install Minima on your iPhone or iPad

← All Guides Android Guide macOS

Prerequisites

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS 14.0 or later
  • At least 100MB of free storage space
  • Stable internet connection (WiFi recommended for initial sync)
  • An Apple ID (required for App Store download)

Step 1: Download Minima

  1. Open the App Store on your device
  2. Tap the Search tab (magnifying glass icon)
  3. Search for "Minima Wallet"
  4. Tap the Get button next to the Minima app
  5. Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password
  6. Wait for the download to complete
💡 Tip

Direct link: apps.apple.com/app/minima-wallet/id6443564057

Step 2: Install the App

  1. Tap the Minima icon on your home screen or in your app library
  2. On first launch, you may see a privacy notice
  3. Tap Continue to proceed
  4. Grant any required permissions when prompted
  5. Wait for the app to initialize

Step 3: Create Your Wallet

When you first open Minima, you'll see the welcome screen.

Option A: Create New Wallet

  1. Tap "Create New Wallet"
  2. Minima will generate a secure 24-word recovery phrase
  3. IMPORTANT: Write down your recovery phrase on paper
  4. Tap I've Written It Down
  5. Confirm your recovery phrase by selecting 3 random words
  6. Create a 6-digit PIN or enable Face ID/Touch ID
  7. Your wallet is ready!

Option B: Restore Existing Wallet

If you already have a Minima wallet:

  1. Tap "I Have a Seed"
  2. Enter your 24-word recovery phrase
  3. Confirm each word carefully
  4. Create a 6-digit PIN or enable Face ID/Touch ID
  5. Your existing wallet and balance will be restored
⚠️ Security Warning

Never share your recovery phrase with anyone. Minima staff will never ask for it. Write it down - don't take a screenshot.

Step 4: Sync with the Network

After creating your wallet, Minima will sync with the blockchain.

  1. Keep the app open while syncing
  2. You'll see a progress indicator
  3. Once synced, you can use Minima normally
  4. The node runs in the background even when the app is closed
📱 Battery & Data

Minima is designed to be battery-efficient. It runs silently in the background. If you have data restrictions, you can limit background data in Settings → Cellular → Minima.

Step 5: Find Your Public Key

You'll need your public key for miniMerch setup:

  1. Open the Minima app
  2. Tap the Maxima icon (bottom of screen)
  3. Your public key is displayed at the top
  4. Tap it to copy to your clipboard
  5. It starts with Mx

Alternatively:

  1. Go to Settings (gear icon)
  2. Scroll to My Addresses
  3. Your default address is your public key

Troubleshooting

App won't download

  • Make sure you're signed into the App Store with your Apple ID
  • Check that your device has enough storage
  • Try restarting the App Store

Sync is stuck or very slow

  • Check your internet connection
  • Toggle Airplane Mode on and off
  • Restart the Minima app
  • Make sure your device's date/time is correct in Settings

Face ID / Touch ID not working

  • Go to Settings → Face ID (or Touch ID) → Other Apps
  • Make sure Minima is enabled
  • You can always use your PIN as a backup
