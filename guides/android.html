📱 Android Setup Guide

Install Minima on your Android phone or tablet

← All Guides iOS Guide macOS

Prerequisites

  • Android device running Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later
  • At least 100MB of free storage space
  • Stable internet connection (WiFi recommended for initial sync)
  • A Google account (for Play Store download) or ability to install APKs

Step 1: Download Minima

You have two options to download Minima for Android:

Option A: Google Play Store (Recommended)

The Play Store version is automatically updated and verified by Google.

  1. Open the Google Play Store on your device
  2. Search for "Minima Wallet"
  3. Tap Install
  4. Wait for the download to complete

Option B: Direct APK Download

If you can't access the Play Store, download the APK directly:

  1. Visit minima.global/post/2024/04/10/minima-mobile-launch/
  2. Download the latest APK file
  3. Open the downloaded file
  4. If prompted, enable "Install from unknown sources" in your settings
💡 Tip

The Play Store version is recommended as it handles updates automatically.

Step 2: Install the App

  1. Tap the Minima icon in your app drawer or on your home screen
  2. If you downloaded via APK, you may need to confirm the installation
  3. Grant any required permissions (storage, network) when prompted
  4. Wait for the app to initialize

Step 3: Create Your Wallet

When you first open Minima, you'll see the welcome screen. You have two options:

Option A: Create New Wallet

  1. Tap "Create New Wallet"
  2. Minima will generate a secure 24-word recovery phrase
  3. IMPORTANT: Write down your recovery phrase on paper
  4. Store it somewhere safe - this is your only way to recover your funds
  5. Confirm your recovery phrase by entering 3 random words
  6. Create a 6-digit PIN for quick access
  7. Your wallet is ready!

Option B: Restore Existing Wallet

If you already have a Minima wallet:

  1. Tap "I Have a Seed"
  2. Enter your 24-word recovery phrase
  3. Create a 6-digit PIN for quick access
  4. Your existing wallet and balance will be restored
⚠️ Security Warning

Never share your recovery phrase with anyone. Minima staff will never ask for it. Anyone with your phrase can access your funds.

Step 4: Sync with the Network

After creating your wallet, Minima will sync with the blockchain. This may take a few minutes.

  1. Keep the app open while syncing
  2. You'll see a progress indicator
  3. Once synced, you can use Minima normally
  4. The node runs in the background even when the app is closed
📱 Battery & Data

Minima is designed to run efficiently on mobile. It uses minimal battery and data. You can restrict background data in your phone settings if needed.

Step 5: Find Your Public Key

You'll need your public key for miniMerch setup:

  1. Open the Minima app
  2. Tap the Maxima icon (bottom of screen)
  3. Your public key is displayed at the top
  4. Tap it to copy to your clipboard
  5. It starts with Mx

Alternatively:

  1. Go to Settings → Addresses
  2. Your default address is your public key

Troubleshooting

Sync is stuck or very slow

  • Check your internet connection
  • Try toggling Airplane Mode on and off
  • Restart the Minima app
  • Make sure your device's date/time is correct

App crashes or won't open

  • Clear the app's cache in Settings → Apps → Minima
  • Restart your device
  • Update to the latest version from Play Store

Forgot your PIN

Use your 24-word recovery phrase to restore your wallet in a new installation.
