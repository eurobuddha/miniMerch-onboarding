Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about miniMerch and Minima

About Minima

Minima is a Layer-1 blockchain that runs completely on-device. Unlike other blockchains that require powerful servers or mining hardware, Minima nodes can run on phones, IoT devices, or regular computers. This makes it perfect for truly decentralized applications like miniMerch.

Every Minima node participates in consensus, making the network extremely decentralized and secure. There's no mining or staking - all tokens are generated through running the node and contributing to network security.

Think of it like running a web server - you need to be online to receive customers, but you don't need enterprise-grade uptime for a small shop.

About miniMerch

miniMerch is a decentralized e-commerce solution built on the Minima blockchain. It allows anyone to create a shop that:

  • Runs as a MiniDapp on the Minima network
  • Accepts crypto payments (USDT or Minima)
  • Encrypts customer delivery addresses
  • Uses no third-party servers or intermediaries

Think of it as "Shopify meets Web3" - but without the company that can shut you down.

Anything legal! miniMerch supports:

  • Physical goods - With postal delivery addresses
  • Digital products - With email or download links
  • Services - With delivery instructions

You control shipping options, prices, and product details. You can sell one item or a full catalog.

Customers can pay with:

  • USDT (ERC-20) - A stablecoin pegged to the US Dollar. Good for customers who don't want crypto volatility.
  • Minima (MX) - The native Minima token. Converted at current market price with a small buffer for slippage.

Payments go directly to your Minima wallet address - no intermediary, no payment processor, no escrow. You receive the funds instantly (or after your node syncs the block).

miniMerch fees: None. There's no monthly fee, no transaction percentage, and no setup fee.

Network fees: There are minimal blockchain transaction fees (usually fractions of a cent) for sending orders and payments. These are paid by the customer when they send their payment.

Compared to platforms like Shopify (29/month + 2.9% per transaction), miniMerch is completely free to use.

Yes! Unlike Shopify, WooCommerce, or other platforms where your store can be deleted by the company, your miniMerch shop is tied to your Minima node and the generated MiniDapp files.

As long as you run a node and host your shop files somewhere, your shop exists. There's no company that can:

  • Ban your account
  • Charge unexpected fees
  • Deplatform your business
  • Go bankrupt and lose your data

Buyers see a simple web page (a MiniDapp) that shows:

  • Your product image and description
  • Price in USD (or Minima equivalent)
  • Size/quantity options
  • Shipping options

When they check out, they enter their delivery address, which is encrypted before being sent. Buyers cannot see other customers' addresses, and they cannot see your vendor information beyond what you choose to display.

You share your shop URL directly with customers. Options include:

  • Local network: Share your local IP for testing
  • Public URL: Set up port forwarding or use a static host
  • IPFS: Host your shop files on the decentralized IPFS network
  • Direct distribution: Give the MiniDapp ZIP file to customers to install on their own nodes

Unlike traditional e-commerce, there's no app store or marketplace. You control how customers find and access your shop.

Privacy & Security

miniMerch uses end-to-end encryption for all sensitive data:

  • Customer delivery addresses are encrypted with your public key
  • Only you can decrypt and read delivery addresses
  • Messages between you and buyers are encrypted
  • No third party can access this data

This means even if someone hacks the blockchain or your node, they cannot see customer addresses.

No. Payment transactions go directly on-chain between the customer and your wallet. You see:

  • The transaction amount and currency
  • That payment was received (via your node)
  • Your balance increases

You do not see:

  • Customer wallet addresses (only your order reference)
  • Customer identities
  • Payment method details

Your Minima funds: Without the 24-word recovery phrase, you cannot access your Minima wallet. This is why it's critical to write it down and store it securely.

Your miniMerch shop: The shop files are generated locally. If you lose them, you can regenerate them from your miniMerch configuration. Keep a backup of your config.json file.

Order history: Stored in your local node's database. Without a backup, you won't be able to see past orders.

Technical Questions

A MiniDapp is like a web app, but with a crucial difference:

  • Regular website: Runs on servers owned by a company (AWS, Google, etc.)
  • MiniDapp: Runs inside the user's Minima node

This means:

  • No server costs
  • No server to hack
  • No company that can shut it down
  • Payments are built into the protocol

To update product info or prices:

  1. Run mini-merch generate again
  2. Enter new product details
  3. Replace the old shop.mds.zip with the new one
  4. Re-install on your node or redistribute to customers

Note: Existing orders are not affected by shop updates - they're tied to the original transaction.

Yes! You can create multiple miniMerch shops for different products or businesses. Each shop generates its own MiniDapp files and can have different:

  • Products
  • Prices
  • Shipping options

All orders from all shops will appear in your miniMerch Inbox.

Minima is a decentralized blockchain - it doesn't have a single point of failure. If some nodes go offline, the network continues.

If Minima as a project were to fail entirely:

  • Your Minima tokens would be unrecoverable (like any cryptocurrency)
  • Your miniMerch shop would stop working
  • But your product inventory and business relationships remain yours

This is a risk with any platform - centralized or decentralized. We believe Minima's technical design makes this outcome very unlikely.
